Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 2026 • shopademia.store

At Shopademia ("we", "our", or "us"), operating at shopademia.store, we take your privacy and the security of your personal information very seriously.

1. Information We Collect

When you purchase or create an account, we collect necessary contact information including your full name, phone number, delivery address, and email address.

Account passwords are stored as password hashes, not plaintext. We use your email address to verify a new account and, when requested, to send a time-limited password-reset link.

2. Payment Data Security

We do not store your credit card details or PINs. For bKash, Nagad, and Rocket transactions, we use the transaction identifier (TrxID) you provide to verify payment status.

3. Retention and Account Deletion

You may request account deletion from your profile. The customer account and active customer tokens are removed; personal contact details linked to past orders are anonymized, while order items, totals, and transaction records may be retained for store, accounting, and legal records.

4. Security and Service Logs

To limit abuse and protect accounts, the service records security events and hashed forms of client IP addresses. These hashes are not guaranteed to be anonymous. The hosting provider may also retain server access and error logs under its own retention settings.

5. Cookies and Browser Storage

The storefront uses necessary browser storage for shopping and account features. Optional analytics, marketing, and session-replay technologies are off unless you choose otherwise; no optional tracking scripts are currently loaded. You can review details or change your choice using Privacy settings or our Cookie Policy.

When you accept, reject, or customize optional privacy categories, a consent receipt records the choice, time, page path, browser locale and timezone, and a coarse device and browser classification. The receipt does not store a raw IP address, raw user-agent, or precise location.

6. Contact & Inquiries

If you have any questions concerning our privacy practices, you may reach our data protection officer at privacy@shopademia.store.

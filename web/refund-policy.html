Refund & Return Policy

7-Day Customer Protection Guarantee

Shopademia applies a return and exchange policy based on the item condition, order details, and current support process. Eligibility and refund handling are confirmed by the support team before a return is approved.

1. Eligibility for Returns

  • The item must be in its original condition, unworn, unused, with tags attached and in its original brand packaging.
  • For fashion items (Panjabis, Kameez), sizing exchanges follow the return arrangements confirmed by our support team.
  • Perfumes and fragrance bottles must have unbroken seals for return eligibility due to hygiene standards.

2. Refund Methods

Approved refunds are processed according to the payment method used for the order and the current return confirmation process. The support team confirms the final payment method and timing after review.

3. How to Initiate a Return

Contact our support team via WhatsApp at +880 1560-024777 with your order number and photos of the item. We will confirm the return arrangements.

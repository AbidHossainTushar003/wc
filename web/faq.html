We accept Cash on Delivery (COD) and mobile payments via bKash Personal (01560024777), Nagad Personal (01560024777), and Rocket Personal (01560024777).

What is your return and exchange policy?

We provide a 7-day hassle-free return and exchange warranty. If you experience sizing issues with Panjabis or clothing, or receive a defective item, simply message our WhatsApp support or submit a return request.

How do I track my active order?

Visit our Live Order Tracking page and enter your Order Number (e.g. WC-10021) and the phone number used when placing the order to see its dispatch status.

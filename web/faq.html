Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Answers to common questions about ordering, delivery, and payments.

How are delivery estimates determined?

Available delivery options, fees, and estimated times are shown during checkout.

What payment methods do you accept?

Available payment options are shown during checkout and may include Cash on Delivery and selected mobile wallet methods depending on your order.

Can I open and inspect the package before paying?

Please review the final delivery and payment choices in checkout before confirming the order. For questions, contact our support team.

What is your return and exchange policy?

Return and exchange eligibility depends on the current policy and item condition. Please contact the support team with your order details for the latest guidance.

How do I track my active order?

Visit our Live Order Tracking page and enter your Order Number (e.g. WC-10021) and the phone number used when placing the order to see its dispatch status.

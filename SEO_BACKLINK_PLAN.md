# Wonderful Collection backlink plan

1. **Complete accurate business profiles.** Verify the store's real name, domain, service area, contact details, and policies before submitting to Google Business Profile (if eligible), relevant Bangladesh business directories, and local commerce associations. Do not publish placeholder phone, payment, or street-address details.
2. **Ask existing partners for relevant citations.** Contact product suppliers, delivery partners, and legitimate business collaborators. Offer a concise “where to buy” or partner reference that links to the relevant public catalog or policy page; request no keyword-stuffed anchor text.
3. **Publish link-worthy customer resources.** Create useful Bangladesh-focused guides based on verified store policies—for example, delivery and payment explanations or gift-selection guides. Link them from the relevant category and FAQ pages, and share them with local publishers and community organizations where genuinely useful.
4. **Build a small, qualified outreach list.** Track publication, contact, relevance, requested resource, response, and resulting URL in a private spreadsheet. Prioritize a handful of local lifestyle and shopping publications over bulk outreach.
5. **Review monthly in Search Console.** Check referring domains, indexed pages, branded queries, and referral traffic. Disavow nothing automatically; investigate suspicious links and avoid paid link schemes, private blog networks, reciprocal-link farms, and automated submissions.

Use only business details the owner has confirmed. Links should be editorially earned and useful to readers, not purchased for ranking manipulation.
